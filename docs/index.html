01 / Architecture

Books in. Decisions out.
A supervisor over both.

The engine is a deterministic hot path. The supervisor is a separate process with the authority to arm, demote and restart flat — never the authority to touch an open position.

Engine

Sub-second book, spot tape, four independent lane budgets, a single frozen decision path. Every window roll re-reads the venue and re-slices equity.

Supervisor

No market decisions. Only evidence and risk: the forward gate, the one-contract canary, the flat restart, the trailing floor, the reconciliation delta.

Dashboard

React 19 + Vite. Lanes, equity curve, calibration, fill forensics and the exact reason a lane abstained — served from /api/state.

05 / Risk

The supervisor can say no.
